What we measure

Nothing on this site measures you.

What the app may measure in future has not been decided. When it is, the list will be here first.

Today

  • This site sets no cookies, runs no scripts and loads nothing from any other website. The people who host it can see ordinary server requests, and nothing more.
  • The app sends nothing to analytics or advertising services.
  • Your family’s activity log records who changed what, so the family can see it. It is part of your record, not a measurement of you.
  • Our server logs exist to keep the service running. Sign-in codes and the contents of messages we send are never written to them.

Before anything is measured

This page will list it first:

  • what is counted, in plain words;
  • why we need it;
  • how long it is kept;
  • and that it is counted by us, not by another company.

This page is a placeholder until that decision is made.
