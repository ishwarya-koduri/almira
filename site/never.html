Our promises

What we never do.

Each promise below rests on how Almira is built, not only on our good intentions. Where that is so, we say what it rests on.

  • We never move your money.Almira has no way to pay, transfer, buy or sell anything. There is no payment system connected to it.
  • We never hold your money.There is no wallet and no balance with us. Your money stays where it is.
  • We never ask for a bank password.Not to import, not to verify, not ever. When bank data can be brought in, it will be through India’s Account Aggregator system, which exists so that nobody has to hand over a password.
  • We never let a role see your private records.Not an admin, not the person who set up the family. The database refuses the read.
  • We never tell someone a record exists if they may not see it.They get “not found”, the same answer as for a record that was never there.
  • We never keep your figures on a shared computer.The web version remembers its own screens so it opens quickly, but never your amounts. When you sign out, they are not left behind.
  • We never write the sign-in codes we send you into our logs.A test follows every path a code takes to prove it.
  • We never contact the person you name as holding a key.That line is sealed on your device. We cannot read it.
  • We never follow you around the web from this site.No cookies, no scripts, no analytics, and nothing loaded from anyone else.

One thing we are careful not to promise

We do not say “we cannot read your data”. That is true only of what you seal. Most of what you record is readable by our server, so that totals and reports can work, and it is protected by the locks on the security page instead.
